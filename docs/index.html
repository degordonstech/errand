03

It watches your back.

It reads agreements before you sign and explains letters and bills. And when you are not sure a product is genuine, snap the pack: MyPaddy reads the registration number, looks it up on the official government register, and shows you exactly what it checked.

  • Reviews agreements before you sign them
  • Explains letters and bills in plain words
  • Flags registration numbers that belong to a different product
  • Spots expired packs and lapsed registrations

04

It makes things for you.

Birthday cards in the colours you pick, with a message written for your friend. A clean product photo for your status from one messy snap. Invoices and neat PDFs from a voice note.

  • Never forgets a birthday
  • Cards with your friend's photo, in your colours
  • Studio-style product photos for your side hustle
  • Invoices emailed to customers after your YES
05

It remembers, so you don't have to.

Facts, people, birthdays, lists, notes and files. Save your tenancy agreement once and ask it questions later. Ask what it knows about you any time, and delete anything.

  • Meter numbers, matric numbers, where you kept the key
  • Shopping and to-do lists, by voice
  • Ask questions of your saved documents
  • Learns about you as you chat
